**Hello! My name is Thomas!**

I have been working at the Centre for Data Processing at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz since 2010.
In 2018 I was appointed head of software development there and, together with my team, I am responsible for various applications related to studying and university administration.
Since 2014 I have also been primarily responsible for operating the authentication and authorisation infrastructure based on Microsoft ADFS, Shibboleth IdP, and several OpenID Connect servers.
In 2022 we began making parts of our software available on GitHub as open-source software.

In addition to my day job, I have been self-employed on a part-time basis since 2011, selling my expertise in software development.
My experience also gave me the idea of making small products available as open-source (but not necessarily free) software.
This is how [ACME-PS](/products/acme-ps/) and the [ACME-ADCS Server](/products/acme-adcs-server/) came about.


## What can I offer?

I would describe myself as an expert in .NET — especially C#, ASP.NET Core, Blazor, Razor, and Entity Framework, as well as PowerShell.
I also have in-depth knowledge of WPF, WinForms, MAUI, Xamarin, HTML, (S)CSS, TypeScript, Angular, React, JavaScript, and SQL.
In addition, I have solid experience operating IIS (as an application and reverse proxy), Apache, nginx, MS SQL Server, AD FS (as a primary authentication server and federation gateway), Shibboleth (as a federation gateway), OpenID Connect (using [IdentityServer](https://github.com/Duende/) and [OpenIddict](https://github.com/OpenIddict/openiddict-core)), and Microsoft Azure.
I have also gained experience with Hugo (this website), C++, Java, Go, YARP, Caddy, WordPress, Moodle, PHP, SharePoint, Jetty, Tomcat, and much more.
In my day job I am also responsible for the login and authentication infrastructure, which has recently been migrated to FIDO2 passkeys.
